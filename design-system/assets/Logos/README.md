The CroustyLab crab. `crab-logo.svg` is the full-colour mark for cream and paper grounds (claws `crab-claw`, body `crab-body`, legs `brick`, eyes `ink`). `crab-logo-mono-brick.svg` is single-ink brick for one-colour use on cream; `crab-logo-mono-cream.svg` is single-ink cream for brick and ink grounds. `app-icon.svg` — cream crab on a brick rounded square with an ink outline — is the favicon and app icon only. Never redraw, outline or recolour the mark beyond these variants.
